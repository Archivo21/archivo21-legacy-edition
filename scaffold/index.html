Archivo 21 Project Scaffold

A practical starting structure for serious passion-led research, preservation and reconstruction projects.

Two downloads are offered here. The standard package is the approved release. The smaller floppy-friendly edition is a convenience derivative for DOS-era filenames and text handling; it does not replace the standard package.

The floppy edition includes FILEMAP.TXT, a derivative MANIFEST.TXT, a tiny FAVICON.GIF and ATTRIB/PROVEN.TXT. These additions document the conversion and its Archivo 21 provenance.

What it is for

The Scaffold helps move a substantial idea or endangered body of knowledge towards a project another person can understand, inspect and continue. It does not require every project to become an institution.

Boundary

Projects retain their own names, identities, domains, governance and version choices. Use of the Scaffold does not imply Archivo 21 support, review, approval or affiliation.
