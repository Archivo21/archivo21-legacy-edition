Floppy-friendly file

The complete Scaffold is a 50,169-byte ZIP file. It contains all 45 release files and fits comfortably on a standard 1.44 MB floppy disk.

  • Download the floppy-friendly ZIP using the verified main-site downloader
  • Open the no-JavaScript release page, checksum and reconstruction instructions
  • Read the revised creation process
  • Open the main-site edition

What it contains

  • project identity, scope and success templates;
  • source and evidence records;
  • decision and work logs;
  • output, release and archive tools;
  • trigger-based governance and continuity records;
  • attribution and non-endorsement material;
  • a completed fictional mixed-media pilot.

Boundary

Projects retain their own names, identities, domains, governance and version choices. Use of the Scaffold does not imply Archivo 21 support, review, approval or affiliation.

Open use

The documentary materials are released under CC BY 4.0, subject to the exclusions recorded inside the package. Commercial use, redistribution and adaptation are permitted. Adaptations may remain private.
