Compiler Desk — Withdrawal memo

LEGACY-MEMO / PUBLIC BOUNDARY CORRECTION

What happened

An early public implementation of the Compiler Desk used an active, person-directed working case as its demonstration before explicit approval had been established for that publication use. The intended interaction model was suitable; the chosen example was not.

Correction

The pages were removed from the live Legacy Edition and from its current source and generated corpus. They were replaced by this self-contained, fictionalised demonstration.

Why there is no public page archive

Publishing an unchanged archive, mirror, screenshot set or downloadable copy would repeat the disclosure that the correction was meant to end. The abstract design, redacted memo and generic replacement preserve accountability without republishing the withdrawn pages.

Private archival record

Files containing the complete withdrawn implementation and its working context continue to exist in restricted private archives for provenance, accountability, recovery and authorised review. They are deliberately neither linked nor reproduced on this site.
