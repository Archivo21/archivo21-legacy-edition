Two bodies of knowledge

The project connects simmers, builders, modders and technical researchers with ANHQV enthusiasts whose detailed knowledge of characters, dialogue, continuity, locations, production and cultural context can become valuable research evidence.

ANHQV is not decoration applied to a Sims project, and The Sims 2 is not merely a passive display tool. The series supplies the cultural world. The game supplies an interactive laboratory in which that world can be reconstructed, tested and deliberately pushed beyond ordinary play.

Research method

Observe → Question → Hypothesise → Design the smallest useful test → Record → Review → Document → Revise

The full repository contains governance, evidence standards, field reports, engine documentation, lore, architecture, Sim design, assets, source records and release planning.

First concluded experiment

EXP000 — In-Game Recording Test

The project’s first recording trial established that the game’s built-in recorder could make short clips, but not footage of sufficient fidelity or reliability for the main research workflow.
