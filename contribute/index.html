ANHQV-TS2 / RECRUITMENT OPEN

Come help rebuild the world of Desengaño 21

ANHQV-TS2 is recruiting.

If you know Aquí no hay quien viva by heart, spend far too long in The Sims 2 build mode, enjoy testing strange engine behaviour, or simply want to help preserve how this work was made, there is useful work you can do.

Who might enjoy it

  • Simmers who build, mod, troubleshoot, document or experiment;
  • ANHQVers who notice dialogue, continuity, locations, props and production details;
  • people interested in preservation, architecture, research, rights, translation or careful criticism; and
  • curious contributors willing to distinguish evidence, memory and interpretation.

You do not need to know every part of the project. One precise correction, one useful source, one reproducible test or one well-documented build can move the work forward.
