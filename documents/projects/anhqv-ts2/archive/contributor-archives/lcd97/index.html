Background

lcd97 founded the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project to preserve, study, reconstruct, and make inhabitable the world of Aquí no hay quien viva through The Sims 2.

He combines deep cultural familiarity with ANHQV and direct access to the live Sims 2 research environment. His work bridges two communities that do not naturally overlap:

  • simmers who may know little about ANHQV;
  • ANHQV enthusiasts whose detailed, apparently useless knowledge can become valuable research evidence.

The project treats both bodies of knowledge seriously. ANHQV is not merely decoration applied to a Sims 2 project, and The Sims 2 is not merely a passive display tool. The series provides the cultural subject, characters, spaces, continuity, tone, and emotional purpose. The game provides the laboratory in which those elements can be reconstructed, tested, documented, and deliberately broken to discover how the simulation works.

ANHQV Knowledge

lcd97 brings long-standing and highly detailed familiarity with Aquí no hay quien viva, including knowledge that might otherwise survive only as informal fandom memory.

Relevant areas include:

  • characters and relationships;
  • episode events and continuity;
  • dialogue, recurring jokes, and character voice;
  • the organisation and use of Desengaño 21;
  • visual and spatial details of sets and locations;
  • cultural context and audience memory;
  • recognition of inconsistencies, ambiguities, and production compromises;
  • the distinction between what is shown, what is implied, and what fans have assumed.

His ANHQV knowledge is especially valuable when converted from recollection into documented claims linked to sources, uncertainty, and reproducible project decisions.

The Sims 2 Knowledge

lcd97 is the project's primary live-environment operator.

Relevant capabilities include:

  • launching and operating the research environment;
  • building and modifying lots;
  • creating and configuring Sims;
  • reproducing observed game behaviour;
  • testing hypotheses during gameplay sessions;
  • recording outcomes, screenshots, and unexpected events;
  • breaking ordinary gameplay assumptions in controlled ways;
  • comparing simulated behaviour with ANHQV source material;
  • identifying when a result reflects the game, a mod, a build choice, or operator intervention.

The project should continue to document not only successful methods but also failed experiments, accidental discoveries, unstable techniques, and the exact ways in which the game was pushed beyond ordinary play.

Motivation

lcd97's purpose is not simply to recreate a television set or produce a themed neighbourhood.

The project aims to:

  • preserve ANHQV as a cultural and emotional reference point;
  • allow people to explore a world that has spoken to them personally;
  • introduce simmers to ANHQV through an interactive reconstruction;
  • transform obscure fan knowledge into useful, testable, reusable research;
  • produce an unusually thorough guide to how The Sims 2 behaves;
  • document how the game was modified, exploited, or broken to build that knowledge;
  • create a repository whose institutional memory survives individual contributors.

Working Style

lcd97 works through a mixture of close observation, experimentation, humour, discussion, and iterative documentation.

Strengths include:

  • strong project vision;
  • cultural memory and source sensitivity;
  • willingness to test ideas directly;
  • comfort with strange or unconventional research questions;
  • ability to notice when a technically correct result feels wrong for ANHQV;
  • preference for documentation that remains useful without becoming sterile.

Useful limitations to record:

  • live testing depends on available Sims 2 sessions;
  • informal knowledge must be converted into cited or clearly qualified records;
  • enthusiasm can generate more research branches than can be pursued immediately;
  • documentation review is most effective when performed during repository integration.

Research Principles

lcd97's work is guided by the following principles:

  • Evidence before authority.
  • Honest uncertainty is preferable to confident invention.
  • ANHQV and The Sims 2 are inseparable parts of the project's mission.
  • A failed experiment is still evidence when properly recorded.
  • Canonical documentation should preserve why a decision was made.
  • Contributors may disagree without becoming adversaries.
  • The purpose of recruitment is to increase the quality of the project, not merely its headcount.
  • Knowledge that once appeared irrelevant can become valuable when organised, tested, and shared.

Sims-Style Biography

lcd97 has spent years collecting knowledge that polite society considered useless: who lived where, who said what, which flat connected to which landing, and exactly why a particular recreation feels wrong even when every wall appears to be in the correct place. Rather than discard this information, he built a research project around it.

Armed with The Sims 2, a live testing environment, and a willingness to break both the game and ordinary standards of productivity, lcd97 is trying to make Desengaño 21 inhabitable. His current wants include documenting everything, recruiting interesting people, and remembering to save before the next experiment.
