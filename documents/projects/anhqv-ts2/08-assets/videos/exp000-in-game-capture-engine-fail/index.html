EXP000 — In-Game Capture Engine Failure

Status: Concluded Outcome: The built-in recorder is unsuitable as the project's main capture method Documentation status: Retrospective and limited

EXP000 tested whether The Sims 2 built-in video recorder could produce footage suitable for research documentation.

Three clean test copies survive. Their direct platform URLs are reserved for controlled review, except for the original This will be your FAVE Short embedded as public evidence on Archivo 21's EXP000 page.

The original movie012.avi recording is held locally and omitted from GitHub.

Its path, size, duration, checksum, and publication status are recorded in the parent video inventory.

The surviving copies demonstrate the recorder's limitations. They must not be used to imply a conclusion for EXP001.
