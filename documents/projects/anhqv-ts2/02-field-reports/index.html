Field Reports

Field reports document individual experiments and investigations.

Each mature report should identify:

  • the research question;
  • environment and configuration;
  • procedure;
  • observations;
  • evidence;
  • limitations;
  • conclusions and confidence;
  • new questions;
  • related media and source records.

A report may remain marked Draft or Ongoing while evidence is incomplete.
