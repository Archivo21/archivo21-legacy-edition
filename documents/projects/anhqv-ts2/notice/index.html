The full legal code is provided in LICENSE.

Attribution should identify the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project (ANHQV-TS2) and the named contributor or contributors where the file or Git history supplies them. Adaptations must be marked and shared under the same or a compatible ShareAlike licence.

Why CC BY-SA 4.0

The project wants its original guides, research structures, explanations, and verified knowledge to circulate widely among simmers, ANHQV fans, educators, archivists, video creators, and future preservation projects.

Attribution preserves provenance. ShareAlike helps improvements remain available to the communities that made the work possible. The licence does not add a non-commercial restriction, because such a restriction can block otherwise valuable reuse, preservation, publication, and compatibility with open knowledge projects.
