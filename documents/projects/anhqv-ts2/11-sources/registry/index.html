Source Registry

This registry is the master human-readable index of sources formally recognised by the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project.

Detailed descriptions belong in individual source records.

Status Key

  • Active — currently available or usable.
  • Unavailable — known source that cannot currently be accessed.
  • At risk — access or continued existence appears uncertain.
  • Archived — at least one documented capture exists.
  • Superseded — retained for provenance but replaced as the preferred source.
  • Withdrawn — removed from active project use for legal, ethical, or reliability reasons.
