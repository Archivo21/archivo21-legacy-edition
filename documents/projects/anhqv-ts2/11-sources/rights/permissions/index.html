Permission Records

This directory contains records of permission requested from or granted by rights holders, operators, creators, participants, or authorised representatives.

Use ../../Templates/permission-record-template.md.

Permission records should preserve:

  • who was asked;
  • what exact material was covered;
  • what exact uses were requested;
  • the response and its date;
  • conditions, exclusions, expiry, and withdrawal terms;
  • where the original evidence is stored.

A friendly or enthusiastic response is not automatically permission for every form of copying or redistribution. Record the actual scope.
