Cultural and Technical Foundations

This project exists because of:

  • the writers, cast, crew, producers, broadcasters, archivists, and fan community of Aquí no hay quien viva;
  • the developers, artists, modders, toolmakers, documentarians, and player community surrounding The Sims 2;
  • generations of forum posts, tutorials, experiments, arguments, jokes, and obsessive observations that preserved knowledge before this repository existed.

Specific sources and contributors should be credited in the documents, source records, experiment histories, and Git history to which they contributed.

The project does not claim ownership of ANHQV, The Sims 2, or third-party work.
