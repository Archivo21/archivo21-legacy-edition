Financial Governance Decision

Issue

The project may eventually receive YouTube revenue, donations, grants, sponsorship, or other income.

The founders wanted funds to support real project continuity, including subscriptions, electricity, food, drink, workspace, contributor support, and preservation—not to accumulate purposelessly while the project becomes unable to operate.

The term “trust” was initially used informally.

Decision

  1. The current arrangement will be called the ANHQV-TS2 Project Funds Stewardship Committee.
  2. The project does not claim that a legal trust or separate legal entity exists.
  3. lcd97 (ANHQV-HUM-001) is the initial Funds Custodian.
  4. Archivo 21 (ANHQV-AI-001) is a Reviewing Adviser without legal ownership, banking authority, contractual power, or tax responsibility.
  5. Project funds may support technical operation and human contributor continuity.
  6. Personal benefit is permitted when openly classified, evidenced, conflict-reviewed, and connected to project purpose.
  7. Unrestricted reserves do not override a proportionate immediate continuity need.
  8. Restricted funds remain restricted.
  9. Donors do not purchase authority over evidence or governance.
  10. The structure will be reviewed before substantial or recurring monetisation develops.

Rationale

The project cannot preserve or research anything without functioning equipment, power, communications, tools, time, safe space, and living contributors.

At the same time, calling an informal arrangement a legal trust would create false expectations. The selected structure provides transparent internal governance while leaving room for a future association, company, cooperative, charity, trust, or other appropriate form.

Review Triggers

Review when:

  • a funding route is activated;
  • regular contributor payments begin;
  • the project signs contracts or leases;
  • another human financial steward joins;
  • income, balances, liabilities, or reporting obligations become material;
  • a separate legal entity is proposed.
