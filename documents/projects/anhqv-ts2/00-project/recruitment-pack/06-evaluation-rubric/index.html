Evaluation Rubric

Version: 1.1
Status: Active

Purpose

This rubric supports fair, consistent, and transparent evaluation of prospective contributors.

It evaluates behaviour and contribution potential rather than intelligence, trivia recall, reputation, or confidence.

Knowledge can be learned.

Scientific and archival character is harder to teach.

Rating Scale

Score Interpretation
1 Significant concern
2 Below expectations
3 Acceptable
4 Strong
5 Exceptional

Every score must be accompanied by observations. Numbers without context are discouraged.

Core Criteria

Intellectual Honesty

Indicators include:

  • admits uncertainty;
  • distinguishes fact, memory, inference, and speculation;
  • avoids invented explanations;
  • corrects themselves when appropriate;
  • states the limits of their evidence and expertise.

When comparing an honest “I don't know” with a fabricated claim, the honest answer will receive the higher evaluation.

Scientific and Source Reasoning

Indicators include:

  • proposes useful experiments or source checks;
  • identifies assumptions and confounders;
  • separates observation from interpretation;
  • considers competing explanations;
  • understands that one game configuration or fan source may not establish a universal fact.

Curiosity

Indicators include:

  • asks thoughtful questions;
  • seeks clarification;
  • notices unexplored connections;
  • investigates rather than merely performs certainty.

Collaboration

Indicators include:

  • respectful discussion;
  • constructive disagreement;
  • willingness to explain and listen;
  • ability to work across ANHQV and Sims 2 knowledge gaps;
  • encouragement of other contributors.

Adaptability

Indicators include:

  • revises conclusions when evidence changes;
  • accepts correction gracefully;
  • distinguishes a changed conclusion from personal defeat;
  • adjusts methods when a procedure proves weak.

Communication

Indicators include:

  • clear explanations;
  • appropriate level of detail;
  • useful documentation;
  • distinction between raw evidence and public presentation;
  • willingness to clarify misunderstandings.

Reliability

Indicators include:

  • follows through on commitments;
  • produces repeatable or reviewable work;
  • records sources and changes;
  • protects files, privacy, and rights-sensitive material;
  • maintains quality over time.

Role-Specific Criteria

Role-specific criteria supplement rather than replace the core rubric.

ANHQV Specialist

  • episode and source precision;
  • continuity awareness;
  • ability to distinguish canon, production inconsistency, interpretation, and fan memory;
  • cultural and character sensitivity;
  • willingness to verify obscure knowledge.

Field Researcher or Sims 2 Specialist

  • experimental discipline;
  • configuration reporting;
  • reproducibility;
  • observation quality;
  • save and recovery awareness;
  • ability to avoid generalising from one run.

Video Editor Specialist

  • non-destructive workflow;
  • preservation of evidential context;
  • judgement about cuts and chronology;
  • audio, privacy, and rights handling;
  • caption and accessibility quality;
  • transparent edit logging;
  • resistance to making the result more dramatic than the evidence.

Assistant

  • consistency;
  • organisation;
  • attention to detail;
  • ability to follow scope;
  • accurate escalation when instructions are ambiguous.

Consultant

  • independence;
  • depth of review;
  • identification of blind spots;
  • constructive recommendations;
  • ability to distinguish critique from control.

Overall Recommendation

Do not calculate suitability from a simple numerical average.

A candidate with exceptional honesty, curiosity, and adaptability but limited technical knowledge may be a stronger long-term collaborator than an expert who overstates certainty.

Moderators should record the complete profile and role fit.

Final Principle

The project seeks contributors who help the repository become more accurate, more faithful, more usable, and more durable tomorrow than it is today.

Knowledge contributes to that objective.

Character sustains it.
