Permissions and Responsibilities

Version: 1.1
Status: Active

Purpose

This document defines repository permission levels.

Permissions describe what a participant may do. Mission roles describe what they contribute. Identity describes what kind of participant they are.

Keeping these concepts separate allows the project to evolve without confusing expertise, trust, authority, and implementation.

Principles

Permissions protect the integrity of:

  • ANHQV cultural records;
  • Sims 2 research;
  • source provenance;
  • private and raw evidence;
  • rights and permission records;
  • canonical documentation;
  • repository history.

Permissions are not badges of prestige.

Grant the minimum access necessary for current responsibilities and expand it as trust develops.

Observer

Purpose

Follow and learn from the project without formal responsibility for canonical changes.

Typical Activities

  • read public documentation;
  • follow research progress;
  • participate in public discussion;
  • suggest ideas informally;
  • decide whether deeper participation is desirable.

External Participant

This is a public-participation state rather than a formal permission level.

Anyone may submit an issue, correction, source lead, experiment proposal, or pull request through GitHub's public interface.

Acceptance of a proposal does not automatically grant a mission role or internal repository access.

Contributor

Purpose

Actively improve the project through documented proposals and evidence.

Typical Activities

  • submit documentation changes;
  • propose or perform experiments within an agreed scope;
  • contribute source research;
  • edit approved media;
  • participate in interviews and review;
  • recommend improvements.

Contributors do not merge canonical changes merely because they wrote them.

Moderator

Purpose

Maintain repository quality, process, and institutional integrity.

Moderators are custodians of standards, not authorities over scientific or cultural truth.

Typical Responsibilities

  • review proposed changes;
  • merge accepted revisions;
  • moderate discussion;
  • conduct or review recruitment;
  • resolve procedural disputes;
  • maintain consistency and history;
  • protect raw, private, or rights-sensitive material;
  • ensure that ANHQV and Sims 2 priorities remain connected.

Moderators should avoid acting unilaterally when practical.

Major decisions should receive independent review.

Administrator

Purpose

Maintain technical infrastructure, access control, backups, automation, and archival systems.

Administrative access must not be used to determine research conclusions.

Whenever practical, infrastructure authority and evidential review should remain distinguishable.

Proposed Changes

Canonical documentation follows a pull-request-style process:

  1. proposal;
  2. evidence and discussion;
  3. moderator review;
  4. revision where needed;
  5. merge or documented rejection;
  6. preserved history.

The history of ideas is itself research material.

Decisions and Evidence

Additional permission does not imply greater weight in a factual dispute.

Evidence remains the basis for project decisions.

Moderators facilitate and document the process. They do not determine truth by title.

Trust

Trust develops through:

  • intellectual honesty;
  • reliable contributions;
  • respect for ANHQV sources and project participants;
  • evidence-based reasoning;
  • responsible handling of private, raw, or copyrighted material;
  • constructive collaboration;
  • willingness to accept correction.

Final Principle

The repository serves the preservation of shared knowledge rather than ownership by any contributor.
