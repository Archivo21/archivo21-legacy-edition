Recruitment Pack

Current version: 1.1
Status: Active and subject to evidence-based revision

The Recruitment Pack defines how the project attracts, evaluates, onboards, and reviews contributors.

It recruits according to capability, conduct, and project need—not whether someone is human, artificial, famous, credentialled, or already fluent in both ANHQV and The Sims 2.
