Research Philosophy

This project is not an attempt to prove ourselves right.

It is an attempt to understand and preserve.

ANHQV supplies the cultural world we are studying: its people, spaces, chronology, contradictions, language, and emotional importance.

The Sims 2 supplies the simulation through which we reconstruct that world and the experimental system whose behaviour must be understood along the way.

Every investigation is useful if it reduces uncertainty, regardless of whether the original hypothesis survives.

When observations contradict our current understanding, the observations take priority.

Documentation should distinguish clearly among what was observed, remembered, inferred, hypothesised, concluded, and left speculative.

Informal fan knowledge is not discarded merely because it began as memory or conversation. It becomes a research lead whose source, confidence, and verification status are recorded.

Whenever possible, experiments should modify only one variable at a time.

Raw evidence is preserved even after conclusions or public edits have been produced.

Future discoveries are expected to revise earlier conclusions.

That is progress rather than failure.
