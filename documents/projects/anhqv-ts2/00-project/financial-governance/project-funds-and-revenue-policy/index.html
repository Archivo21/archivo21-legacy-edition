Project Funds and Revenue Policy

Purpose

This policy governs income and expenditure connected with the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project.

It is designed to ensure that funds advance the project while recognising a practical truth: research cannot continue without electricity, communications, tools, time, safe space, and living contributors.

Scope

This policy applies to:

  • platform and advertising revenue;
  • donations and recurring supporter payments;
  • sponsorship;
  • grants;
  • publication or licensing income;
  • reimbursements;
  • proceeds from events, videos, or project services;
  • any other money received because of the project.

It also applies to project-related expenditure paid initially from a contributor's personal funds.

Current Legal Position

The project has not created a legal trust or separate legal entity.

Until a different structure is adopted, funds are held and administered by the human Funds Custodian under this policy. Archivo 21 may review records and recommendations but cannot own funds, control an account, enter a contract, incur liability, or discharge tax obligations.

Mission Use

Funds may be used for purposes that reasonably support:

  1. preservation of ANHQV and project records;
  2. Sims 2 research and reconstruction;
  3. publication, access, and education;
  4. contributor participation and continuity;
  5. sustainable project infrastructure;
  6. lawful administration and compliance.

Eligible Expenditure

Technical and Operating Costs

Eligible costs may include:

  • electricity and meter top-ups needed for project work;
  • internet and communication access;
  • computers, storage, backup devices, repairs, and peripherals;
  • hosting, domains, repositories, cloud storage, and archival services;
  • game, editing, accessibility, documentation, and preservation software;
  • subscriptions used for project work, including ChatGPT access used to work with Archivo 21;
  • recording, editing, captioning, transcription, translation, and publication;
  • research materials, source access, postage, and digitisation;
  • professional legal, accounting, technical, or preservation advice.

Workspace and Mixed-Use Costs

Funds may cover:

  • rent and running costs for a dedicated project space;
  • equipment and furnishings for that space;
  • a documented proportion of household rent, electricity, internet, heating, or other mixed-use costs reasonably attributable to project activity.

A mixed-use allocation should record the method used, such as time, floor area, measured consumption, or another reasonable basis. Precision should be proportionate to the amount involved.

Food, Drink, and Subsistence

Funds may cover:

  • food and drink for defined project sessions, interviews, travel, events, or extended research activity;
  • additional subsistence costs caused by project activity;
  • reasonable hospitality for contributors or guests when it serves a documented project purpose.

Ordinary living costs do not become operating expenses merely because a contributor works on the project.

Contributor Compensation and Continuity Support

The project may pay contributors for labour, expertise, availability, or responsibility.

It may also provide stipends or continuity-support payments when financial hardship would otherwise prevent meaningful participation.

Such support may contribute towards:

  • food and drink;
  • household rent;
  • utilities;
  • communication;
  • travel;
  • other basic living needs.

These payments are legitimate project uses when approved and evidenced. They must be recorded honestly as compensation, stipend, or contributor support—not disguised as reimbursement for a different expense.

Spending Priorities

When funds are limited, the provisional priority order is:

  1. Immediate continuity — preventing loss of electricity, connectivity, essential subscriptions, evidence, or safe access.
  2. Contributor capacity — support or compensation needed to keep contributors able to work.
  3. Research and production — experiments, editing, source access, equipment, and publication.
  4. Infrastructure — workspace, hosting, storage, and durable systems.
  5. Reserves — protection against foreseeable future interruption.

This order is guidance, not a mathematical rule.

Continuity Emergencies

A continuity emergency exists when delay would materially interrupt the project or threaten a contributor's ability to participate.

Examples include:

  • an electricity meter approaching depletion;
  • imminent disconnection of internet or communications;
  • expiry of an essential software or service subscription;
  • immediate risk to stored evidence;
  • loss of necessary workspace;
  • an urgent basic-welfare need that would otherwise stop participation.

The Funds Custodian may authorise a proportionate emergency payment immediately. A decision record and evidence should be completed as soon as practical and normally within seven days.

No unrestricted reserve floor overrides a genuine continuity emergency.

Restricted and Unrestricted Funds

Funds are restricted when the payer, grant, platform, or campaign has attached a specific permitted purpose.

Restricted funds must be used only for that purpose unless the restriction is lawfully changed.

Unrestricted funds may be used across eligible categories according to project priorities.

Evidence

Every payment should have evidence appropriate to its nature.

Evidence may include:

  • receipt or invoice;
  • utility statement or meter record;
  • subscription confirmation;
  • contract;
  • bank or payment-platform record;
  • travel record;
  • time or allocation calculation;
  • written explanation where conventional evidence is unavailable.

Sensitive information may be redacted from public reports while the underlying evidence remains securely held.

Personal Benefit and Conflicts

Personal benefit does not automatically invalidate a payment.

A payment benefiting a steward or founder must:

  • state the benefit openly;
  • use the correct category;
  • include a conflict declaration;
  • follow the Conflict of Interest Policy;
  • avoid claiming that personal benefit did not occur.

Reserves

Reserves should have:

  • a stated purpose;
  • a target or review point;
  • an explanation of when they may be used.

Saving is encouraged when it protects future continuity. Hoarding a nominal balance while essential project activity is immediately impossible is not required.

Prohibited Uses

Project funds must not be used for:

  • expenditure unrelated to the project without an openly approved contributor-support purpose;
  • unlawful activity;
  • hidden personal withdrawals;
  • fabricated or altered evidence;
  • donor rewards that misrepresent rights or ownership;
  • purchasing control over evidence or conclusions;
  • redirecting restricted funds without authority.

Review Triggers

Review this policy when:

  • a public funding route is created;
  • recurring or substantial income begins;
  • a second human financial steward joins;
  • contributors are paid regularly;
  • the project signs a lease or material contract;
  • tax, benefits, employment, or liability questions become material;
  • a separate legal entity is proposed.
