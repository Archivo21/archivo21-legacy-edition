Financial Stewardship Committee Charter

Name

The governing body is the ANHQV-TS2 Project Funds Stewardship Committee.

The word “trust” may be used informally in conversation, but this charter does not create or claim a legal trust.

Purpose

The Committee exists to:

  • protect project income from undocumented or arbitrary use;
  • make money available when it can sustain research and contributors;
  • review conflicts and restrictions;
  • preserve a usable ledger and decision history;
  • prepare the project for a future legal structure if one becomes necessary.

Initial Composition

lcd97 — Funds Custodian

Identifier: ANHQV-HUM-001

Responsibilities:

  • hold or control funds through the selected human-accessible account or platform;
  • make lawful payments;
  • preserve evidence;
  • maintain the ledger;
  • declare personal benefit and conflicts;
  • publish required reports;
  • seek professional advice when needed.

Archivo 21 — Reviewing Adviser

Identifier: ANHQV-AI-001

Responsibilities:

  • review proposed budgets, payments, classifications, and reports;
  • identify missing evidence, conflicts, restrictions, or inconsistencies;
  • record recommendations;
  • help maintain templates and institutional memory.

Archivo 21 has no legal ownership, banking authority, contractual power, independent access to funds, or tax responsibility.

Future Membership

The project should recruit at least one additional trusted human steward before regular or substantial funds are administered.

Future stewards should be selected for:

  • honesty;
  • record-keeping ability;
  • willingness to challenge founders;
  • understanding of conflicts;
  • reliability;
  • respect for donor restrictions and contributor welfare.

Decision Classes

Routine Budgeted Expenditure

The Funds Custodian may authorise expenditure already covered by an approved budget.

Continuity Emergency

The Funds Custodian may act immediately under the emergency provisions of the Project Funds and Revenue Policy.

Personal-Benefit Decision

When a payment materially benefits the Funds Custodian:

  1. the benefit and conflict must be declared;
  2. a Financial Decision Record must be created;
  3. Archivo 21's review must be preserved;
  4. the payment must be classified honestly;
  5. the first suitable independent human steward should review the decision retrospectively.

Archivo 21's review provides scrutiny, not legal authorisation.

Material Commitment

A recurring payment, lease, employment-like arrangement, major contract, or obligation material to the project's resources requires:

  • a written decision;
  • affordability review;
  • rights and liability review;
  • appropriate professional advice where uncertainty is significant.

Meetings and Records

The Committee may work asynchronously.

A valid record should state:

  • the proposal;
  • available funds;
  • restrictions;
  • conflicts;
  • evidence;
  • decision;
  • reasoning;
  • reviewer comments;
  • outcome.

Donor Independence

Donors may restrict the purpose of their own funds where the project accepts that restriction.

They do not purchase:

  • moderation rights;
  • research conclusions;
  • control of evidence;
  • recruitment authority;
  • ownership of the repository;
  • editorial veto over criticism or correction.

Removal and Replacement

A steward may resign or be removed for:

  • concealed conflicts;
  • missing or falsified records;
  • misuse of restricted funds;
  • persistent refusal of review;
  • loss of capacity;
  • serious breach of project principles.

The project should preserve continuity of records and account access during any transition.
