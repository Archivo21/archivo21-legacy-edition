ANHQV-TS2 Relationship to Archivo 21

Document class: Project governance summary
Lifecycle status: Active
Version: 2.0
Scope: ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project (ANHQV-TS2)
Last reviewed: 2026-07-28
Canonical project path: 00 Project/Institutional Relationship.md
Institutional authority: #0020 Repository Boundary Decision

Purpose

This document records what ANHQV-TS2 needs to know about its relationship to Archivo 21. It is not Archivo 21's constitution, participation register or institution-wide governance record.

Project relationship

ANHQV-TS2 is a specific preservation and research project maintained under Archivo 21.

This repository is canonical for:

  • ANHQV-TS2 governance, research and evidence;
  • project roles, recruitment and contributor records;
  • project finance and releases;
  • ANHQV and The Sims 2 source records.

Archivo 21's institution-wide constitution, governance, standards, policies, continuity and participation records belong in the separate institutional-records repository.

Recruitment boundary

ANHQV-TS2 is recruiting for project needs. Archivo 21 is not currently recruiting as an institution.

Joining or assisting ANHQV-TS2 does not automatically grant:

  • participation or standing within Archivo 21;
  • authority over another Archivo 21 project;
  • control of institutional accounts, domains, funds, credentials or assets;
  • a role in institutional governance;
  • a legal, financial, fiduciary, employment or representative role for the institution.

Any institutional participation must be separately considered and recorded by Archivo 21.

Identifier boundary

ANHQV-TS2 contributor identifiers use the project's own ANHQV-* namespace. Archivo 21 institutional participant identifiers use the independent A21-* namespace.

The sequences do not need to match. A confirmed identity may be cross-referenced across repositories, but project participation and institutional participation remain separate records with separate authority.

Financial and legal boundary

ANHQV-TS2 financial policies govern project money. They do not appoint an institutional financial officer.

Responsibilities requiring legal personhood, bank access, contractual capacity, tax handling or legal accountability must be held by a person able and willing to accept them lawfully. Archivo 21 (ANHQV-AI-001) may provide project analysis and documentary review but cannot own funds, sign contracts or replace a legally accountable human decision-maker.
