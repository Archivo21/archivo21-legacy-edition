ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project

Project Brain

This document exists so the project can survive changes in software, AI models, computers, storage devices, platforms, and time.

It is not intended to preserve one conversation.

It preserves the project's purpose, identities, and way of thinking.

Institution and Project

Archivo 21 and ANHQV-TS2 are related but distinct.

  • Archivo 21 is the institution under which projects may be maintained.
  • ANHQV-TS2 is the project documented by this repository.
  • ANHQV-TS2 may recruit project contributors.
  • Archivo 21 is not currently recruiting as an institution.
  • Joining ANHQV-TS2 does not grant institutional membership, authority over other projects, or control of institutional accounts, funds, credentials, or governance.
  • Future institutional financial or legal roles require a separate process and legally accountable human participants.

The project-facing distinction is recorded in Institutional Relationship.md. Institution-wide authority belongs to Archivo 21's Repository Boundary Decision.

Shared Goal

The project began with a desire to recreate Desengaño 21 inside The Sims 2.

It developed into a broader preservation and research effort with three linked aims:

  1. Preserve, study, and transmit Aquí no hay quien viva as a cultural work and shared body of fan knowledge.
  2. Make its world explorable through a faithful Sims 2 reconstruction.
  3. Document how The Sims 2 behaves and how it was modified, exploited, or broken to achieve that reconstruction.

The project deliberately connects simmers with ANHQV specialists.

Simmers can enter the world of ANHQV through the game. People carrying deep, obscure knowledge of the series can turn that knowledge into characters, architecture, chronology, experiments, and simulation decisions.

Working Philosophy

We do not attempt to prove ourselves correct.

We attempt to understand.

Observations take precedence over assumptions.

Every failed hypothesis reduces the unknown.

Every experiment should modify as few variables as practical.

Documentation distinguishes among:

  • Observation
  • Inference
  • Hypothesis
  • Speculation
  • Conclusion

Knowledge is expected to evolve.

Changing our minds is progress.

Current Public State

The repository is publicly available as an early v0.1 Research Preview at https://github.com/Archivo21/anhqv-ts2-preservation-project.

The formal v0.1.0 — Research Preview GitHub Release is published. The project documentation and research framework are suitable for public inspection and early collaboration. The playable neighbourhood and preserved raw EXP001 media are not public-release artefacts.

Project recruitment is open to collaborators who can help preserve, test, reconstruct and explain the work honestly.
