1. Purpose

This register reconstructs Archivo 21's institutional document chronology, assigns the one-time permanent sequence permitted by #0017 and identifies each document's current canonical version.

It does not number every source, capture, experiment, project document or ephemeral note. Those retain their own identifier systems.

2. Numbering rule

This is the initial chronology reconstruction.

On adoption on 2026-07-28:

  • the numbers below become permanent;
  • revised versions retain their number;
  • numbers are never reused;
  • future substantial institutional documents receive the next available number;
  • CONT-001, EXTERNAL-AI-001, contributor IDs, source IDs and experiment IDs remain distinct namespaces.
