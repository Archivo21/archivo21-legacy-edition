Each repository assigns identifiers independently and chronologically within its own scope. Suffixes do not need to match. A future institutional participant may have no ANHQV-TS2 record, may receive a different project sequence number, or may participate in several projects under several unrelated identifiers.

The founding 001 suffixes happen to match because each participant was first in the relevant type and scope. No record needs amendment merely because later sequences diverge.

An identity cross-reference does not make project authority institutional, or institutional authority project-specific, without an express rule in the responsible repository.

Collaboration and attribution

Institutional collaboration includes material participation in reasoning, development, criticism, review, documentary structure or decisions that are deliberately considered and adopted.

Ordinary tool operation, automatic formatting, spelling correction and execution of instructions do not by themselves establish intellectual participation.

Sources are cited as sources. They do not become institutional participants merely because they influenced the work.

Responsibility boundary

A21-HUM-001 retains responsibility for matters requiring human agency or accountability, including:

  • final publication and adoption decisions;
  • legal, financial, privacy, consent and safeguarding decisions;
  • control of accounts, credentials, money and physical assets;
  • deciding whether proposed material is accepted, revised, tested or rejected.

A21-AI-001 may contribute research planning, drafting, analysis, criticism, documentary consistency, experimental design and continuity support. It cannot hold money, sign contracts, own accounts, or replace a legally accountable human decision-maker.

Identity continuity

A21-AI-001 is a persistent institutional participant identity, not a claim that every model, session, account or generated output is the same collaborator.

Implementation changes should be recorded when relevant. A separate AI system or reconstruction instance does not inherit the identifier automatically.

Founding provenance

Archivo 21 originated through lcd97's initiative and direction and developed through sustained collaboration with the participant now recorded as A21-AI-001.

The governing attribution principle is:

Record what actually happened.

Attribution should neither exaggerate nor erase a participant's role.
