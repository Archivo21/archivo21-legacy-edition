This log distinguishes approved decisions from unresolved proposals. It is not a substitute for the full standards.

Approved

Institutional priorities

  • Preservation has a particularly strong institutional focus.
  • Understanding and accessibility are not secondary afterthoughts; they are integrated with preservation.
  • Working formulation: Preservation is the mission. Understanding is the method. Accessibility is the promise.
  • Charter principles may deliberately overlap and reinforce one another.

Preservation and accessibility

  • When preservation and convenience conflict, preservation takes precedence.
  • Preservation must maintain an accessible form and structure for both humans and artificial intelligence.
  • Context, metadata and documentation form part of preservation.

Charter

  • Charter Article VIII, No Dead Ends, is approved.
  • Negative, abandoned, disproven and inconclusive work remains worthy of preservation.

Style

  • Documentation should respect the reader's time.
  • Repeated routine instructions should have a concise reference form so experienced readers do not reread the same explanation.
  • Full beginner explanations should exist and remain linked rather than being deleted.
  • The Style Guide should prohibit sterile writing without defining natural human writing and artificial-intelligence writing as opposites.
  • Every Page Should Be Worth Finding is approved.
  • Humour is welcome when it does not replace clarity.
  • Standardise Structure, Not Thought is approved as an institutional principle.

Experiment language

  • Replace Why is this experiment necessary? with language centred on why the experiment is interesting or noteworthy.
  • Do not use Failure as the default experiment outcome.
  • Use descriptive outcomes such as Answered, Partially Answered, Unanswered, Inconclusive and Superseded.
  • Experiment status and experiment outcome are separate fields.

Experiment identification

  • Permanent chronological EXP### identifiers are approved.
  • Series IDs are approved to group closely related experiments that may not have adjacent chronological numbers.
  • Related work should not depend on suffixes such as EXP001a or EXP001-2.

Evidence

  • The descriptive Evidence Ladder is approved:
    • Anecdotal
    • Observed
    • Reproduced
    • Independently Verified
    • Established
    • Retired
  • Evidence levels describe confidence, not importance.
  • Retired conclusions remain preserved.

Terminology

  • The Archivo 21 Terminology Standard is approved for drafting.
  • Core definitions include Behaviour, Intended Behaviour, Defect, Side Effect, Limitation, Quirk, Exploit, Myth, Legend and Resolved Legend.
  • Deprecated Terminology belongs in an appendix to the glossary.
  • The glossary includes Canon, Community Canon and Archive Canon.
  • Terminology exists to improve understanding, not to police language.

Project and institution separation

  • The ANHQV-TS2 incident scale remains project-specific:
    • 🟢 Vecinos tranquilos
    • 🟡 Hay movimiento
    • 🟠 Se está liando
    • 🔴 ¡QUÉ FOLLÓN...!
  • That scale is not the Archivo 21 institutional reproducibility scale.
  • Institutional standards should remain useful beyond The Sims 2 and ANHQV-TS2.

Draft status decision

This controlled dump is labelled Draft 0.1.

It consolidates approved ideas but does not yet promote them to institutional canon.
