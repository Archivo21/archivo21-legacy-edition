Archivo 21 Institutional Records

This repository is the permanent canonical home for Archivo 21's institution-wide constitution, governance, standards, policies, continuity records, planning and documentary history.

It is deliberately separate from:

  • Archivo21/anhqv-ts2-preservation-project, which governs and documents the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project; and
  • Archivo21/archivo21.github.io, which builds the public Archivo 21 website and publishes readable HTML representations of records held here.

The approved remote repository is Archivo21/institutional-records. Compiler review on 2026-07-28 authorised its creation after the privacy condition and publication checks recorded in #0019 and #0024 are satisfied.

Authority and publication

Markdown in this repository is canonical unless a record states otherwise. The website is the public reading layer: it renders these files as formatted webpages and links back to their canonical source.

An institutional document's lifecycle status, publication state and canonical location are separate properties. A public draft is not automatically adopted; an internal active record is not automatically public.

Participant identifiers

Institutional participation uses the Archivo 21 namespace:

  • lcd97 — A21-HUM-001
  • Archivo 21 — A21-AI-001

ANHQV-TS2 contributor identifiers remain in that project's independent namespace and do not grant institutional authority. Matching 001 suffixes in the founding records are coincidental; there is no cross-namespace numbering rule.
