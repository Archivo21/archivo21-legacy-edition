These matters are deliberately unresolved. They should not be silently completed or presented as approved canon.

1. Series ID Syntax

Decide the permanent form of a Series ID.

Questions include:

  • mnemonic only, such as FDN;
  • mnemonic plus number, such as FDN-001;
  • institution-wide or project-scoped prefixes;
  • how Series IDs remain unique across projects;
  • whether an experiment may belong to multiple Series;
  • how a primary Series, if any, is chosen;
  • how Series are retired, split or merged without losing history.

2. Archivo 21 Reproducibility Scale

Create institutional names and descriptions that sound like Archivo 21 rather than ANHQV-TS2.

The scale may use cultural references from different areas of preserved digital history while remaining understandable without prior knowledge.

Candidate reference domains raised during drafting:

MSN Messenger

Logging in and trying to change status before contacts reacted.

The historical point must be accurate: contacts could receive the login notification immediately, so the task was quick but not simple and could be lost despite preparation and timing.

The Sims 2

Restoring Bella Goth to Pleasantview without damaging the neighbourhood.

The wording must distinguish the original Pleasantview Bella from the separate Strangetown Bella character record and should be technically verified against indexed Mod The Sims material before adoption.

Aquí no hay quien viva

Paloma arranging José Miguel's second communion so that Lucía would invite her to a wedding that ultimately never happened.

This represents an elaborate chain of dependencies whose objective may collapse because of events outside the researcher's control.

Open design questions:

  • number of levels;
  • formal level names;
  • whether cultural scenarios are names, subtitles or examples;
  • how preparation, knowledge, timing, risk and recoverability are combined;
  • whether projects may override the institutional scale.

3. Evidence Codes

The descriptive Evidence Ladder is approved.

Decide whether the shorthand codes E0, E1, E2, E3, E4 and ER become permanent or remain internal working notation.

4. Archive Canon Threshold

Decide whether a minimum evidence level is required before a claim may become Archive Canon.

Possible complications include:

  • evidence that is strong but inherently unreproducible;
  • historically unique artefacts;
  • claims whose scope is narrow but thoroughly documented;
  • community history where social acceptance is itself the subject.

5. Status and Outcome Vocabulary

The separation between status and outcome is approved.

Review the working terms for completeness and define whether Superseded may appear as both a status and an outcome.

6. Citation and Referencing Standard

Define:

  • citation forms for webpages, forums, videos, files, conversations and physical material;
  • archived URL requirements;
  • access dates;
  • version and revision references;
  • citation of deleted or inaccessible sources;
  • quotation limits and transcription conventions;
  • machine-readable citation metadata.

7. Media Preservation Standard

Define:

  • source, preservation and access copies;
  • preferred and accepted formats;
  • checksums;
  • naming;
  • metadata;
  • transcoding records;
  • platform-hosted copies;
  • local masters;
  • screenshots, video, audio and project files.

8. Mod The Sims Index

Determine the scope and method for indexing relevant Mod The Sims material.

The index should make future technical answers faster without converting community claims into Archive Canon automatically.

Questions include:

  • which forums, tutorials, downloads and comments are in scope;
  • preservation of URLs and access dates;
  • duplicate and superseded advice;
  • distinction between author claims and independently verified results;
  • relationships between MTS entries, Legends and experiments.

9. Promotion to Draft 1.0

Before promotion:

  • review the consolidated wording;
  • remove accidental duplication;
  • resolve high-impact open questions;
  • check cross-references;
  • decide repository paths and public visibility;
  • record the pre-change repository state;
  • retain a recoverable snapshot for at least four weeks.
