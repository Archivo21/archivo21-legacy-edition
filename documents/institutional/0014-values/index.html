1. Integrity

Archivo 21 values honesty over certainty.

Evidence should be presented faithfully, uncertainty acknowledged openly, and speculation clearly distinguished from established knowledge.

2. Thoroughness

Archivo 21 believes that understanding is strengthened by completeness.

Where practical, subjects should be documented comprehensively rather than superficially.

3. Accessibility

Knowledge should remain discoverable, understandable and usable.

Accessibility means helping people navigate complexity, not removing it.

4. Continuity

Archivo 21 seeks to preserve not only knowledge, but also the methods required to understand, verify and extend that knowledge.

Documentation should reduce dependence on individuals and encourage long-term stewardship.

5. Intellectual humility

Archivo 21 recognises that present knowledge is incomplete.

New evidence may confirm, refine or overturn previous conclusions.

Revision is a strength of the archive, not a weakness.

6. Respect for evidence

Conclusions should follow evidence.

Where evidence is incomplete, the limits of present knowledge should be documented rather than concealed.

7. Respect for time

Archivo 21 recognises that time is a finite resource.

The institution therefore seeks not only to preserve knowledge, but also to preserve the time of those who create, maintain and use that knowledge.

Documentation should be as complete as necessary, but no more burdensome than required to achieve its purpose.

Processes, standards and workflows should minimise unnecessary effort while preserving the quality, integrity and continuity of the archive.

Time is itself a resource worthy of careful stewardship.
